Watchdog — guarded metrics

The guard list. Anything here is monitored whether or not a screen shows it. When a value crosses a limit, a warning triangle pops up over whichever screen is showing, naming the metric, its value and the limit it broke. Tap the display to acknowledge and mute that one fault; an escalation to critical or a second fault re-arms it straight away.


Warning lamps

The car's own telltales — check engine, seat belt, doors, parking brake, oil, charging, temperature, fuel, knock, lights, cruise and the indicators. Each lights on the dash screen and on a strip over the other screens, and the serious ones can pop up the alert card. A lamp whose signal the master never sends simply stays dark, so leaving one on costs nothing. Tap the display to dismiss a pop-up.

Folded, the strip keeps only the most urgent red or amber lamp (with "+N" for the rest) so it never sits over your data for long. Indicators keep it open while they flash; high beam, cruise and lights go after the few seconds - the dash screen shows them all the time.

Seat belt, door and parking brake only pop up and flash when the car is moving; oil pressure and charging are only judged once the engine has been running a few seconds. The three custom lamps are spares for anything found on the bus later (ABS, airbag…).

Swipe animation

LVGL's own default is linear, which is what makes a slide feel mechanical. Easing is what makes it read as smooth.


Master & ESP-NOW network

Read once at boot — reboot to apply.

The channel must match the master exactly. A mismatch produces silence rather than an error, which is a miserable thing to debug — if the link is down, check this first. A MAC filter only matters when more than one master is in range, since ESP-NOW broadcasts carry no notion of which network they belong to.

WPA2 needs at least 8 characters; anything shorter leaves the portal open. Changing these changes the network you are currently connected to — you will have to rejoin after the reboot.


Alerts & input

If the button does nothing, open the DIAGNOSTICS screen on the device, hold the button, and watch the GPIO row — the pin that reads L is the one to put here.


Smoothing & colours

Higher alpha = snappier but noisier. Lower = smoother but laggier.

Asset Uploader

Upload LVGL .bin images for gauge backgrounds (240×240), then reference them in a screen as /assets/name.bin. Convert with the LVGL online image converter using CF_TRUE_COLOR, binary output and RGB565 Swap — the firmware runs with LV_COLOR_16_SWAP = 1, so an unswapped image comes out with red and blue channels mangled. The bundled backgrounds were made by tools/make_assets.py.

Backgrounds are streamed from flash on every repaint, so prefer them on cell/text screens; on a screen with a sweeping needle they cost frame rate.
